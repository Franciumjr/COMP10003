MicroMelb

Microwaves around Parkville campus

The best Microwave Navigator in Melbourne University.

MicroMelb is a free, open-source, and community-driven microwave navigator for Melbourne University's Parkville campus. It is a web application that allows users to find microwaves around the campus and compare them based on their features and specifications.
