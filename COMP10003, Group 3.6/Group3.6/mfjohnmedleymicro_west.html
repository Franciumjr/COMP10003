Directions

From the first stop in this building, go back past the elevator and cross the bridge to the West Tower. Make a left at the end of the corridor, and take the elevator down to floor 1. Exit the elevator and turn right, where you will find the kitchenette.

Comment from the Authors

If the microwave in the East Tower didn't take your fancy, this one sure will! The most notable improvement are the modern aesthetics of the room, coming with plenty of room. This kitchenette has a wide range of amenities, including a fridge, a toasty maker, and of course, a printer! Who wouldn't have printer in their kitchen?
