Ease of access:

Go to the MSD building using any of the entrances, either the front or the back, and proceed to the second level (Level 2) by the stairs or the elevator. Once on Level 2, go to the east side of the building, which is the front side of the building. On the left-hand side there, you will find two microwave ovens. On the 3rd floor, the location of the microwave oven is the same, and on the 4th floor, it is also same as the second and third floor. However, for the 3rd and 4th floors, always go before 6:00 PM. This area is restricted and only a few students who have special access can use these ovens after regular tutorial classes end at 6:00 PM. The microwave oven on the 2nd floor can be accessed anytime when the MSD building is open.
