Directions

Enter West Edge Biosciences (125) through ground floor Eastern entrance. Continue straight, and enter the first room to the left - Student Kitchenette (G03) is here.

General Comments

This newly established, hidden microwave area resides on the ground floor of West Edge Biosciences (125), providing ample indoor seating.

It provides an abundance of beautiful study areas, and is equipped with simple amenities including, six microwaves, and two vending machines, making it a basic location for heating food.

Not busy.
