Directions

Under the welcome sign at the main entrance of the university, turn left and head into the East Tower of the John Medley Building. Turn right as you enter, find the elevator, and take it to the second floor. After exiting the elevator, take 3 quick lefts and you will find yourself in the kitchenette!

Comment from the Authors

This microwave is less than desirable than most, its main limitation being the restrictive area surrounding it, giving it its poor environment and accessibility ratings. It does however make up for these shortcomings in its functionality and nearby facilities. It's a fairly modern design, so easy to use, and the room has a sink as well as a fridge, so a very versatile space.
