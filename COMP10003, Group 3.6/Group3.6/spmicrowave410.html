Directions

Enter Student Pavillion (162) through Southern entrance. Locate, and take the elevator (alternatively, the stairs are beside the elevator) to the 4th (top) floor. Turn left, and take the first corridor to the left - Community Kitchen (410) is immediately in front.

General Comments

This microwave area resides on the top floor of the Student Pavillion (162), providing ample indoor seating areas.

It is well equipped with many amenities including, one microwave, two hot/cold taps and sinks, two ovens, two dishwashers, stovetop, benchside seating, and other kitchen applicanes, making it an ideal location for group lunch settings.

Usually not busy.
