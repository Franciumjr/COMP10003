Directions

Enter Old Agriculture Building (142) through ground floor Eastern entrance. Turn right - Kitchen (G62A) is immediately in front.
Alternatively, enter West Edge Biosciences (125) through ground floor Eastern entrance. Turn left through corridor - Kitchen (G62A) is immediately to the right.

General Comments

This microwave area resides on the ground floor of the Old Agriculture Building (142), providing ample indoor, and some outdoor seating.

It is connected to West Edge Biosciences (125), providing abundant study areas, and is well equipped with amenities including, two microwaves, multi-purpose bin area, hot/cold tap and sink, and dishwasher, making it a niche location for group study lunch settings.

Usually quite busy.
