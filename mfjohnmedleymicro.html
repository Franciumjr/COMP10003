John Medley Building

Microwave #1: East Tower Level 2

Overall Rating: 5/10

Category Rating
Environment 5/10
Accessibility 3/10
Functionality 6/10
Facilities 5/10

Directions

Under the welcome sign at the main entrance of the university, turn left and head into the East Tower of the John Medley Building. Turn right as you enter, find the elevator, and take it to the second floor. After exiting the elevator, take 3 quick lefts and you will find yourself in the kitchenette!

Comment from the Authors

This microwave is less desirable than most, its main limitation being the restrictive area surrounding it, giving it its poor environment and accessibility ratings. It does however make up for these shortcomings in its functionality and nearby facilities. It's a fairly modern design, so easy to use, and the room has a sink as well as a fridge, so a very versatile space.

Microwave #2: West Tower Level 1

Overall Rating: 8/10

Category Rating
Environment 8/10
Accessibility 8/10
Functionality 8/10
Facilities 7/10

Directions

From the first stop in this building, go back past the elevator and cross the bridge to the West Tower. Make a left at the end of the corridor, and take the elevator down to floor 1. Exit the elevator and turn right, where you will find the kitchenette.

Comment from the Authors

If the microwave in the East Tower didn't take your fancy, then this one sure will! The most notable improvement is the modern aesthetic of the room, coming with plenty of space. This kitchenette has a wide range of amenities, including a fridge, a toasty maker, and of course, a printer! Who wouldn't have printer in their kitchen?
